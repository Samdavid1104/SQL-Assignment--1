---Display a Department Id ,average salary of employee where City is chennai and and average salary is > than 650000.From this we need atlease depatrmrnt more than 2 employee.Sort the reults by average salary in descending order

ANSWER
Select DeptID,Avg(Salary) from Employee
Where City ='Chennai'
Group by DeptID
Having Avg(Salary)>650000 and Count(EmpName)>2
Order by avg(salary) Desc;
                                              

28.07.2026------------Task-2

Using the Employee table, display:

Department
Number of employees
Average salary
Total bonus

Conditions:
Consider only employees from Chennai or Bengaluru
Ignore employees whose Bonus is NULL
Show only departments with at least 3 employees
Show only departments where the average salary is greater than 65000
Sort by total bonus in descending order


ANSWER:
SELECT D.DeptName ,COUNT(E.EmpID) ,AVG(E.Salary) ,SUM(E.Bonus) 
FROM Employee E INNER JOIN Department D
ON E.DeptID = D.DeptID
WHERE E.City IN ('Chennai', 'Bengaluru')AND E.Bonus IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY D.DeptName
HAVING COUNT(E.EmpID) >= 3 AND AVG(E.Salary) > 65000
ORDER BY TotalBonus DESC;
